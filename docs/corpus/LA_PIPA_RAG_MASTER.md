# La Pipa: comprehensive workspace record

## Canonical summary

La Pipa is an independent, people-led open-innovation community, working environment, and media/cultural platform historically based in a solid-stone nineteenth-century cider mill in Somió/Gijón, Asturias, northern Spain. The project was conceived as a convergent space where entrepreneurs, designers, technologists, artists, researchers, media makers, and organizations could work together across company building, service design, data, artificial intelligence, privacy, media, culture, music, content, and experiences.

Its own early materials describe it as an independent project, a bet on Gijón, a collaborative and co-managed ecosystem, a flexible space for imagination, and an internationally connected base for research and development. A 2021 presentation describes more than 35 people and five organizations operating at La Pipa in less than two years, with an in-house studio and broadcast capability. Later archive material shows the name extending into La Pipa Studios, La Pipa TV, cultural and music programming, festivals, partner events, research themes, and a digital platform at `lapipa.ai`.

La Pipa should not be reduced to a coworking office. The evidence supports at least four overlapping identities:

1. **A place:** a cider-mill property used as offices, meeting rooms, studios, event space, gardens, and technical workspace.
2. **A community:** an independent collective of founders, companies, collaborators, creators, and visitors.
3. **A method and platform:** open innovation built around trust, collaboration, shared intelligence, human-centred design, and cross-disciplinary work.
4. **A media and cultural producer:** audio, video, interviews, podcasts, live broadcasts, music sessions, festivals, talks, workshops, and creative experiments.

## When La Pipa started

The earliest strong, directly inspected origin document is the 36-page `LAPIPAPROJECT` presentation created on **10 June 2019**. A company-ecosystem chart and a first budget workbook are also dated June 2019. A 2020 strategy presentation repeatedly labels the project “2019 La PiPa,” and an April 2021 document says that, “in less than two years,” La Pipa had grown to more than 35 people and five organizations. Together these sources make **2019 the best-supported practical origin year**.

The exact canonical start date is not resolved. A July 2026 redesign handover explicitly records a conflict: a brand book reportedly says **2016**, while the site and `lapipa.io` reportedly say **2019**. A generated secondary research report gives **29 September 2019**, but its underlying primary citation was not independently inspected in this review.

Use this wording in downstream systems:

> La Pipa is documented no later than 10 June 2019 and is strongly supported as a 2019-era initiative. Its exact legal, operational, and public launch dates remain unresolved because later workspace records conflict between 2016 and 2019.

## Foundational intent and principles

The 2019 origin presentation frames La Pipa as:

- an independent project and a commitment to Gijón;
- internationally connected, with links to innovation laboratories elsewhere in Spain;
- a business opportunity based on converging industries;
- a trust-based partnership between entrepreneurs from Gijón who returned after working elsewhere and people who remained and built locally;
- a Gijón research-and-development base with international reach;
- an ecosystem connecting data, creativity, services, content, culture, media, technology, and experiences.

The 2020 strategy presentation adds the following principles: irreverence; mixing future, present, and past; optimism; positive impact by design; independence; people-led collaboration; self-sufficiency; co-management; flexibility; empathy; privacy by design; circular-economy thinking; listening to learn; and partnership.

## Early scale claims

The June 2019 presentation makes the following unverified project claims: more than ten companies, approximately €45 million in combined turnover, 97 percent of business volume outside Asturias, and more than 150 people across London, Madrid, Barcelona, and Gijón. It also describes a Gijón team of 45 professionals and 12 planned new jobs during 2019.

These numbers are **claims in a planning presentation**, not independently audited results. They should not be used as current metrics.

## Activities documented across the workspace

### Open innovation and business ecosystems

La Pipa hosted and developed work around open innovation, service and company design, founder support, organizational models, value exchange, stakeholder ecosystems, regenerative business, and positive-impact investment. The September 2022 Springboard agenda describes an Asturias gathering at La Pipa involving investment, education, construction, digital product and experience studios, and ecosystem practitioners. Its stated objective was to understand how business is generated in Asturias and to explore purpose-centred ecosystems with economic, social, and environmental benefits.

### Data, artificial intelligence, privacy, and technology

Early materials place data and AI at the centre of an ecosystem connecting people, products, services, brands, content, and connected things. Documented themes include data science, AI, privacy, robotics, the Internet of Things, edge computing, immersive media, virtual and augmented reality, and human-centred technology. A 2021 equipment workbook includes computers, robotics, a Misty II robot, projectors, sound and lighting equipment, and vintage computers and synthesizers. Later archive names refer to analytics, platforms, Media Futures, and technology interviews.

### Service design and professional collaboration

The `LAPIPA _COLLECTIVE001` deck presents a service-design partnership model for building companies, services, and stakeholder value through human data by design. It emphasizes empathy, total experience, privacy by design, positive impact, agile practice, and DevOps. Planning workbooks also model event, service, coworking, hospitality, and partnership activity. Forecasts in these workbooks are planning evidence, not proof of realized revenue.

### Media, broadcast, podcast, and content production

By April 2021, La Pipa described a 24/7 in-house studio and broadcast operation. The archive contains La Pipa TV material, the multi-episode `DEAD PROGRAM AT LA PIPATV`, interviews, open-source talks, live and edited music sessions, partner videos, podcast-related files, studio equipment inventories, and a 2024 audio/video podcast rate-card workbook. This supports a sustained media-production function, not merely occasional event documentation.

### Music, culture, festivals, and live experiences

Music and culture appear throughout the archive. Examples include `RON SINGS AT LA PIPA HACKSPACE 2021`, `LA PIPA FOLK FRANKIE AND SCOTS 2022`, Subterránea-related recordings, Gijón Sound Festival assets, Open Day interviews, Nomad Talks, and the 2023 La Pipa Festival / North of the South / Edition 00 material. These filenames prove the existence of associated media artifacts; event dates and programs require primary promotional or production records before being treated as fully verified.

### Talks, workshops, hackspace, and visits

The archive contains recurring evidence of talks, interviews, workshops, working sessions, open days, visits, and hackspace activity. Named examples include the 2022 Springboard sessions, `ASTURIAS VISIT LA PIPA FEB22`, La Pipa Open Day artifacts, `AVA @ LA PIPA NOMADTALKS`, `NOMADTALKS LA PIPA FESTIVAL`, and several specialist or partner interviews from 2023–2025.

### Sustainability, regenerative models, food, and place

Sustainability appears in the Springboard work on regenerative models and in a January 2023 `LA PIPA NATURAL FOODS` memo about plant-based foods, local production, rural opportunity, affordability, and nutrition. Garden, orchard, food-and-beverage, and circular-economy concepts also appear in planning files and festival artifacts. The natural-foods document is an opportunity memo, not evidence that a food venture was launched.

### Studio and production services

A 2024 cost workbook models La Pipa Studios services including audio podcasts and video podcasts, with labor, equipment, margin, and rate calculations. It establishes that production services were actively costed or offered during 2024; it does not establish sales volume or realized revenue.

## Evidence conflicts and unresolved questions

1. **Start date:** 2019 is best supported, but a 2026 handover records 2016 versus 2019. The generated date 29 September 2019 is not independently verified.
2. **Legal identity:** a secondary generated report names `SERVICE DESIGN ECOSYSTEMS SL` and calls the initiative non-profit. No registry record, deed, bylaws, or primary legal document was inspected.
3. **Physical address and tenure:** the workspace supports Somió/Gijón and a cider-mill setting. Exact title, lease, address, contract, purchase option, and 2026 transaction status require property or contract evidence.
4. **Current status:** `www.lapipa.io` and the archive deployment status were refreshed on 5 August 2026, while the user reported leaving the historical physical offices. A live digital surface does not resolve present physical occupancy or the legal/operational relationships among the site, community, studio, and archive.
5. **Event completeness:** the archive contains many media artifacts, but a single authoritative event calendar, attendee ledger, and outcome report was not found.
6. **Financial performance:** available workbooks are plans, forecasts, cost models, or contribution models. They do not establish audited revenue, costs, investment, or realized contributions.
7. **Organizational relationships:** La Pipa, La Pipa Studios, La Pipa TV, Bedrock, ReMotive, partner companies, the physical property, and `lapipa.ai` should remain separate entities until legal and operational relationships are documented.
8. **Archive duplication:** many of the 2,557 La-Pipa-named paths are Final Cut backups, copies, or exports. Path counts must not be interpreted as unique projects.
9. **Archive deployment identity:** GitHub and Supabase confirm the newly supplied archive connections. The supplied Vercel project ID is not visible under the connected La Pipa team; it may belong to a different team/account, be inaccessible to the connector, or be stale.
