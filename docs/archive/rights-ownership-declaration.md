## Declaration

Alex Lawton states that he and his holding company, **Miramonte, S.L.**, collectively own **100% of the intellectual-property and related rights in La Pipa and in the materials, initiatives, content, archive, documentation, and project assets associated with La Pipa**.

The archive records Alex Lawton and Miramonte, S.L. as the declared rights holders and uses the following credit line unless a more specific approved credit is required:

> © Alex Lawton and Miramonte, S.L. All rights reserved.

This declaration authorizes the La Pipa Documentary Archive to preserve, describe, index, embed, migrate, replicate, provide controlled access to, and prepare documentary uses of project materials, subject to the archive's release controls.

## Evidence classification

This is a direct owner-supplied declaration, recorded verbatim in substance and accepted as the archive's operational rights basis. It is not represented as an independently verified legal opinion, company-registry extract, deed of assignment, contributor agreement, or chain-of-title audit.

The declaration resolves the archive-level copyright-holder question for current operations. It does not erase or pre-decide separate obligations involving privacy, data protection, participant consent, performers' or authors' moral rights, publicity and personality rights, confidential information, third-party contracts, music clearances, trademarks, or material whose rights may have been granted to La Pipa rather than originally created by it.

## Access and publication effect

Rights ownership and access are separate decisions. This declaration permits preservation activity but does not automatically make every record public. Existing access classifications remain in force until the applicable privacy, consent, sensitivity, accessibility, editorial, and release gates pass.

Public release still requires an item-level access decision and an owner-approved release record. Restricted or unavailable provider media remains restricted or unavailable until the relevant technical and ethical conditions are resolved.
