Nº 01

What is La Pipa?

Identity / place / purpose

La Pipa brought entrepreneurs, designers, technologists, artists, researchers, media makers, and organizations together in Gijón, Asturias. Its work crossed company building, service design, data, artificial intelligence, privacy, media, culture, music, content, and shared experience.

The earliest directly inspected project deck is dated 10 June 2019. It is the strongest practical origin point currently in the record. The exact legal, operational, and public launch dates remain unresolved because later materials cite both 2016 and 2019. The archive preserves that uncertainty instead of inventing certainty.

A place for people to make things possible together.

The archive remembers.

Evidence / context / care

A checksum is not a story. A story is not a master file. Both need care.

The source inventory currently spans about 2.3 TB, 188,222 files, and 66,388 directories. Counts include duplicates, exports, and backups; they are an inventory boundary, not a claim of unique works. Originals remain distinct from descriptions, interpretations, embeddings, and documentary edits.

Nº 06

How the record is made.

Documentary / archival / accountable
01 / ORIGINALS

Preserve

Accession, characterize, hash, replicate, verify, and retain the source without silent alteration.

02 / CONTEXT

Describe

Connect objects to people, places, activities, dates, custody, sources, and uncertainty using stable identifiers.

03 / VOICES

Listen

Record oral histories with consent, preserve unedited testimony, and distinguish memory from documentary fact.

04 / EVIDENCE

Verify

Mark claims as verified, attributed, filename-only, unresolved, or contradicted. Never flatten the differences.

05 / DISCOVERY

Connect

Use a provenance graph and access-aware Voyage retrieval to find relationships with citations back to sources.

06 / PUBLICATION

Share responsibly

Resolve rights, privacy, credits, sensitivity, accessibility, correction, and takedown before release.
