Nº 01

What this archive is.

Purpose / responsibility

The La Pipa Documentary Archive preserves the evidence of La Pipa: how it began, who made it, where it happened, what took place, what was produced, and how it is remembered. It brings together archival control, editorial knowledge, a provenance graph, and evidence-aware retrieval.

It is not a folder of disconnected files and it is not a machine-generated history. Original objects remain distinct from descriptions, interpretations, embeddings, and documentary edits. Rights, consent, privacy, sensitivity, and uncertainty travel with every record.

Memory becomes useful when its origin remains visible.

Nº 03

Built to remember.

Preservation / not storage alone

A checksum is not a story. A story is not a master file. Both need care.

Preservation records the object, its context, custody, rights, technical character, transformations, and every integrity check. Originals are never silently overwritten. Derivatives always point back to their source.

02SHA-256 at capture and ingest

Nº 04

Documentary method.

From object to accountable story
01 / RECEIVE

Accession

Record source, authority, custody, extent, restrictions, and a transfer manifest before managed ingest.

02 / PROTECT

Preserve

Quarantine, scan, characterize, hash, store, replicate, verify, and retain the original bitstream.

03 / UNDERSTAND

Describe

Connect items to people, places, activities, subjects, chronology, sources, and uncertainty.

04 / LISTEN

Transcribe

Align voices to time, identify speakers carefully, keep review states, and preserve unedited testimony.

05 / CLEAR

Review

Resolve rights, consent, privacy, sensitivity, credits, access tier, and potential present-day harm.

06 / TELL

Publish

Release citations, accessible media, context, and corrections without severing the evidential chain.
